## 0. THE PHYSICS OF THE FILL
**Notes:** A fill is a "kinetic bridge." It increases the density of notes to build pressure, which is then released when the next section begins.

1.  **The "1" Rule:** No matter how complex the fill, you must land a heavy **Bass (B)** on the "1" of the following measure.
2.  **Hand Height:** Build volume by gradually increasing the height of your hands (The "Crescendo").
3.  **Stay in the Pocket:** Do not speed up the tempo during the fill. Increase the *density* of notes, not the *speed* of the clock.

## 1. COMMON WESTERN FILLS

### [ 1 ] The "16th-Note Build" (Verse to Chorus)
**Notes:** Used in the last measure of a pre-chorus. It starts soft and ends with a loud Flam (Pla) to "drop" the chorus.

**Notes:** A rolling fill that creates a "tumbling" sensation. This uses 16th-note triplets (4 pulses of 3).

## 2. SEQUENCE-OF-OPERATIONS: THE DROP
1.  **Section A:** Maintain the "Standard Kit" pattern (Western Pop Version 1.1).
2.  **The Fill:** Execute one of the above patterns in the *final* measure of Section A.
3.  **Section B:** Land a **Bass (B)** strike with maximum resonance on the first beat of the new section.
