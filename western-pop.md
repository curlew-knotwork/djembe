## 0. THE WESTERN CONTEXT: THE BACKBEAT
**Notes:** When playing with a Western band, you are the "Clock." Traditional djembe dances around the beat; Pop djembe defines the beat. Your primary objective is the **Backbeat** (the hit on 2 and 4).

1.  **Lock the Snare:** Your Slap (S) must land exactly with the snare drum or the clap.
2.  **Simplify:** In Pop, less is more. Do not over-fill the space; leave room for the instrumentation and vocals.
3.  **Dynamics:** Use soft "Ghost Tones" for verses and open up for the chorus.

## 1. TECHNICAL SPECIFICATION: SOUND & PHYSICS
**Notes:** The djembe acts as a mechanical replacement for a standard drum kit.

| Type | Code | Phonetic (R/L) | Physical Action |
| :--- | :---: | :--- | :--- |
| **Bass** | **B** | GUN / DUN | **Kick Drum:** Center strike. Flat palm, immediate rebound. |
| **Tone** | **T** | GO / DO | **Tom/Filler:** Edge strike. Pads of fingers closed. Round sound. |
| **Slap** | **S** | PA / TA | **Snare Drum:** Edge strike. Relaxed fingertips for a sharp crack. |
| **Flam** | **Pla**| **PLA** (Both) | **Cymbal/Crash:** Both hands hit together for maximum accent. |
| **Rest** | **-** | (Silence) | **Ghost Note:** Hand moves in air to maintain the pendulum. |

## 4. REPOSITORY NOTES
* **The "1" Rule:** Land a heavy Bass (B) on the first beat of the next section after any fill.
* **Pendulum Maintenance:** The R-L-R-L alternating stroke is the physical clock; do not break it during fills.
