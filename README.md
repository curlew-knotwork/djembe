# djembe
Some notes about playing Djembe drum, tablature, etc.

# 1. DJEMBE TECHNICAL NOTATION & PHONETIC SPECIFICATION
====================================================================
VERSION: 1.0 (Unified Physical & Oral Notation)
TIMING: 4/4 Common Time (16th-Note Subdivisions)
====================================================================

[ I. SOUND DEFINITION & PHYSICS ]
--------------------------------------------------------------------
TYPE   | CODE | PHONETIC (R/L) | PHYSICAL ACTION
--------------------------------------------------------------------
BASS   |  B   |  GUN  /  DUN   | Center: Flat palm, immediate rebound.
TONE   |  T   |  GO   /  DO    | Edge: Fingers closed, hit rim with pads.
SLAP   |  S   |  PA   /  TA    | Edge: Fingers relaxed, "whip" motion.
REST   |  -   |  (Silence)     | Air-stroke: Maintain pendulum motion.
--------------------------------------------------------------------

[ II. UNIFIED 16-SLOT GRID ]
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Beat:      | 1  e  &  a  | 2  e  &  a  | 3  e  &  a  | 4  e  &  a  |
Hand:      | R  L  R  L  | R  L  R  L  | R  L  R  L  | R  L  R  L  |
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Phonetic:  | GUN -  GO DO | PA -  TA -  | GUN -  GO DO | PA -  TA -  |
Sound:     | B  -  T  T  | S  -  S  -  | B  -  T  T  | S  -  S  -  |
--------------------------------------------------------------------

[ III. VERTICAL ZONE STAFF (MAP TO DRUM SURFACE) ]
--------------------------------------------------------------------
           | 1  e  &  a  | 2  e  &  a  | 3  e  &  a  | 4  e  &  a  |
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Slap (S):  | -  -  -  -  | S  -  S  -  | -  -  -  -  | S  -  S  -  |
Tone (T):  | -  -  T  T  | -  -  -  -  | -  -  T  T  | -  -  -  -  |
Bass (B):  | B  -  -  -  | -  -  -  -  | B  -  -  -  | -  -  -  -  |
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Hand (H):  | R  L  R  L  | R  L  R  L  | R  L  R  L  | R  L  R  L  |
--------------------------------------------------------------------

[ IV. SEQUENCE-OF-OPERATIONS & CONSTRAINTS ]
--------------------------------------------------------------------
1. THE PENDULUM RULE: The arms must move in a continuous R-L-R-L 
   cycle. Even during a rest (-), the hand must descend and 
   ascend in "ghost time" to preserve the micro-timing.
   
2. LEAD-HAND ANCHOR: The Right Hand (R) is the primary driver for 
   downbeats (1, 2, 3, 4) and upbeats (&). The Left Hand (L) 
   handles the "e" and "a" subdivisions.
   
3. REBOUND PHYSICS: Never "press" or "hold" the hand on the skin
--------------------------------------------------------------------

# 2. # DJEMBE REPERTOIRE: COMMON PATTERNS SPECIFICATION
# ====================================================================
# KEY: B=Bass | T=Tone | S=Slap | - =Rest
# HANDS: R=Right | L=Left (Dominant hand on 1, &, 2, &)
# ====================================================================

[ 1. KUKU (4/4 - Common Time) ]
# Origin: Forest Guinea. A celebratory rhythm.
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Beat:      | 1  e  &  a  | 2  e  &  a  | 3  e  &  a  | 4  e  &  a  |
Hand:      | R  L  R  L  | R  L  R  L  | R  L  R  L  | R  L  R  L  |
Sound:     | B  -  S  -  | B  -  S  -  | B  B  T  T  | S  -  S  -  |
Phonetic:  | GUN -  PA -  | GUN -  PA -  | GUN DUN GO DO | PA -  TA -  |
--------------------------------------------------------------------

[ 2. MORIBAYASSA (4/4 - Common Time) ]
# Origin: Malinke. Played for women who have fulfilled a difficult vow.
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Beat:      | 1  e  &  a  | 2  e  &  a  | 3  e  &  a  | 4  e  &  a  |
Hand:      | R  L  R  L  | R  L  R  L  | R  L  R  L  | R  L  R  L  |
Sound:     | B  -  T  T  | S  -  -  -  | B  -  T  T  | S  -  -  -  |
Phonetic:  | GUN -  GO DO | PA -  -  -  | GUN -  GO DO | PA -  -  -  |
--------------------------------------------------------------------

[ 3. SOLI (12/8 - Triplet Feel) ]
# Origin: Malinke. Traditionally played during rites of passage.
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Beat:      | 1  .  .  | 2  .  .  | 3  .  .  | 4  .  .  |
Hand:      | R  L  R  | L  R  L  | R  L  R  | L  R  L  |
Sound:     | B  -  -  | T  -  T  | S  -  S  | T  -  T  |
Phonetic:  | GUN -  -  | DO -  GO | PA -  TA | DO -  GO |
--------------------------------------------------------------------

[ 4. DJOLE (4/4 - Common Time / Mask Dance) ]
# Origin: Temne people (Sierra Leone/Guinea border).
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Beat:      | 1  e  &  a  | 2  e  &  a  | 3  e  &  a  | 4  e  &  a  |
Hand:      | R  L  R  L  | R  L  R  L  | R  L  R  L  | R  L  R  L  |
Sound:     | B  -  B  -  | T  T  S  -  | B  -  B  -  | T  T  S  -  |
Phonetic:  | GUN -  DUN - | GO DO PA -  | GUN -  DUN - | GO DO PA -  |
--------------------------------------------------------------------

[ NOTES ]
--------------------------------------------------------------------
1. VERTICAL ALIGNMENT: Every strike (Sound/Phonetic) is physically 
   mapped to a specific 16th-note or triplet slot.
2. HAND COHERENCE: The R-L-R-L sequence is never broken, even 
   during complex phrases, ensuring the "physics of the groove" 
   remains consistent across all patterns.
3. SUBDIVISION SHIFT: Note that Soli (12/8) uses 3 slots per beat 
   instead of 4, changing the feel from "square" to "swing."
--------------------------------------------------------------------
